# Original HQ dispatch recordings (0.14.5)

Original Abandoned Salvaging dialogue authored for the dispatch telephone. Speech was rendered locally
with the installed macOS system voice and processed into a copper-line telephone sound using the
standard-library authoring script at Tools/Audio/generate_hq_dispatch.py. No audio pack, sample,
voice download, paid service, or recording of a real person was used.

Runtime terms and crew-scaled quota remain in the selected job's visible transcript; these fixed clips
speak safety warnings only. The telephone shows their exact spoken words even with sound muted.

## dispatch_routine

Abandoned Salvaging dispatch. Review the job sheet before departure. Keep the crew together, listen for floor warnings, and load salvage into the truck. Bring everyone home.

## dispatch_power_off

Abandoned Salvaging dispatch. This job has no power. Bring charged flashlights and keep the crew together. Review the job sheet, and listen for floor warnings.

## dispatch_high_threat

Abandoned Salvaging dispatch. This is a high threat job. Expect more monsters, sooner. Keep noise down, read the field guide, and bring the crew home together.

## dispatch_sealed

Abandoned Salvaging dispatch. Access is shuttered on this job. Pack bolt cutters or a crowbar before departure. Review the job sheet, and leave room for salvage in the truck.
