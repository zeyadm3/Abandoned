# ART, SOUND AND FONTS
Furniture Kit 2.0 by Kenney (www.kenney.nl) (CC0 1.0): Loot models (laptop, TVs, coffee machine, ...), store props.
Car Kit 3.1 by Kenney (www.kenney.nl) (CC0 1.0): The HQ van, parked vehicles in the mall lot.
Impact Sounds by Kenney (www.kenney.nl) (CC0 1.0): Loot impacts per material, footsteps, the plank snap and collapse debris.
Interface Sounds by Kenney (www.kenney.nl) (CC0 1.0): Menu clicks, confirm/back/error, open/close.
RPG Audio by Kenney (www.kenney.nl) (CC0 1.0): Structure creaks/groans, cloth (pickup), pocketing, coins (payday, the Collector), latch (truck lever), click (flashlight), distant settling.
Kenney Fonts by Kenney (www.kenney.nl) (CC0 1.0): Menu text until the UI overhaul (now unused).
Game Icons by Kenney (www.kenney.nl) (CC0 1.0): Menu icons (settings, audio, crew, trophy, warning, exit...).
Generic Items by Kenney (www.kenney.nl) (CC0 1.0): Gear icons (flashlight, walkie-talkie, medkit, bolt cutters...).
Board Game Icons by Kenney (www.kenney.nl) (CC0 1.0): HUD symbols (skull, dollar, hourglass, lock, award...).
Input Prompts by Kenney (www.kenney.nl) (CC0 1.0): Mouse button glyphs in key prompts.
Barlow, Barlow Condensed by Jeremy Tribby (The Barlow Project Authors) (SIL OFL 1.1): Body text (Barlow), headings and buttons (Barlow Condensed).
Noto Sans, Noto Sans Arabic by The Noto Project Authors (Google) (SIL OFL 1.1): Russian headings, and names and chat in Cyrillic, Greek and Arabic (0.19.9).
Noto Sans SC, Noto Sans JP, Noto Sans KR (Medium) by Adobe and Google (The Noto CJK Authors) (SIL OFL 1.1): The Chinese, Japanese and Korean interface, and names and chat in those scripts (0.24.8; they replace the variable-weight SC and KR files of 0.19.9).
Saira Stencil One by Omnibus-Type (The Saira Stencil Project Authors) (SIL OFL 1.1): Big titles and stamps only.
IBM Plex Mono by IBM Corp. (SIL OFL 1.1): The appraisal receipt.
VT323 by Peter Hull (The VT323 Project Authors) (SIL OFL 1.1): The HQ terminal.
Kokoro-82M speech model by hexgrad (Apache-2.0): The voices of the boss's voicemails and the dispatch telephone.
Meridian environment kit by ABANDONED original art (Project-owned original work): 52 custom modular architecture, prop, decay and mystery FBX assets; 21 procedural PNG textures.
Threat rigs and animation by ABANDONED original art (Project-owned original work): Eight custom skinned threat silhouettes, Idle/Walk/Chase/Attack/Special animation takes and procedural grime.
Horror sound design by ABANDONED original audio (Project-owned original work): Engine, siren, roar, whispers, ceiling movement, wall knocking, area water/vent sounds and threat signatures.
Salvage paperwork texture by ABANDONED original art (Project-owned original work): Procedural worn paper and grime for readable horror menus.
Menu film grain, vignette, scratches, stain and glow by ABANDONED original art (Project-owned original work): 0.12.2 procedural textures for the horror menus' grain, vignette, worn title and button glow.
Surface grain and soft particle maps by ABANDONED original art (Project-owned original work): 0.12.2 procedural maps for previously untextured materials.
Wardrobe and HQ props by ABANDONED original art (Project-owned original work): 0.12.5: 14 hats and 11 accessories fitted to the crew worker, the HQ answering machine, 11 more procedural palette textures.
World text shader by ABANDONED original code (Project-owned original work): Single-sided, depth-tested sign and label text.
Meridian realistic architecture and structure kit by ABANDONED original art (Project-owned original work): 83 deterministic FBX exports: six floor finishes with crack/sag variants and nine-piece fracture siblings; ten-piece flight fractures; walls, storefronts, columns, balustrades, escalators, stairs, haul ramp, ceilings, skylight, entrance canopy, facade, fixed retail fixtures, cinema, dock and decay props; major models carry LOD0/1/2 groups.
Meridian original PBR surfaces by ABANDONED original art (Project-owned original work): 25 procedural PBR sets / 75 PNGs: original terrazzo aggregate, tile/mortar, carpet weave, concrete pores, plaster, brushed metal, brass trim, ceiling perforations, wood grain, rust, glass, water and small dressing surfaces; 2048 hero, 1024 standard, 512 small props/decals; base colour, normal and packed metallic/occlusion/smoothness mask.
Meridian fictional sign and graffiti designs by ABANDONED original art and writing (Project-owned original work): Original sign frames, worn notice silhouettes and non-offensive abstract graffiti; fictional store/wayfinding text uses the existing Abandoned/WorldText shader and credited fonts.
Meridian overcast night sky by ABANDONED original art and code (Project-owned original work): Deterministic 512 px cloud texture and textured URP sky shader for the mall exterior.
Meridian parking and collection markings by ABANDONED original art and writing (Project-owned original work): Parking bays, crosswalk, directional arrows, utility lamps, original crew-van lettering and collection/cash-out notices; material variants reuse the original Meridian PBR maps.
Meridian detailed fitted fixtures by ABANDONED original art (Project-owned original work): Fourteen original modules: slatted concourse bench, mall bin, blank advertising lightbox, empty clothing rack/hangers, retail gondola, checkout counter, food booth, food service station, cinema screen, ticket counter, empty wire service cart, stock cage, ceiling duct and shuttered kiosk. LOD0/1/2 meshes share the original Meridian PBR maps.
Everything else (synthesised sounds, ambience, greybox) was made for the game.
