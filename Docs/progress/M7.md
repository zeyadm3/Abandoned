### 7.1 notes (art pass)
- Imports: `Art/ThirdParty/Kenney/<Pack>/` with the pack's License.txt. `ThirdPartyModelImport`
  (AssetPostprocessor) strips animation/cameras/lights/colliders and keeps materials in the FBX.
- Loot: `LootDefinition.Model` (+ `ModelYaw`). `LootModelBuilder.AssignMissing` (in rebuild) gives
  definitions without a model their Kenney model and resizes `Size` to hug it (keeping the authored
  largest side). Only empty definitions are filled, so a model chosen in the inspector is kept.
  `LootPrefabGenerator` keeps the Size box as the collider (renderer removed) and fits the model
  inside it (`ModelFit.Place`). Items without a model keep their placeholder shape.
- 13 new loot items (household + electronics) with models and spawn tags; the catalog grew to match.
- Mall set dressing: `MallProps` puts themed Kenney fixtures (shelves, counters, desks, sofas,
  plants, boxes) against store walls, in a 0.85 m strip clear of loot points, doors, exterior openings,
  flights and jackpot tiles; seeded by tile. Each has one box collider and a `SectionProp` that hides it
  when its floor collapses and shows it again on restore (cosmetic, not networked, no load).
- Vehicles: the HQ van is dressed with the Car Kit van (box keeps its collider + HqVan); two parked
  vehicles in the mall lot. The mall's company truck stays greybox for now: its walk-in cargo bay and
  ramp are gameplay geometry and no CC0 model matches them.
- `NetworkLootHitTests.ClientCarriedItemStrikingRestingLootDamagesAndPushesItOnTheHost` now checks the
  largest movement after the strike: the modelled TV (deeper box) rocks back onto its base instead of
  sliding, so its final position is unchanged although the push works. `LootStrikes.PushDirection`
  now pushes along the floor (a hit on an item's top used to push it down into the floor).
- Screenshots: `M7_*` (store interiors, parking, loot lineup via `ArtPreview`).
