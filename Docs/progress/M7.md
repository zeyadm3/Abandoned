### 7.1 notes (art pass)
- Imports: `Art/ThirdParty/Kenney/<Pack>/` with the pack's License.txt. `ThirdPartyModelImport`
  (AssetPostprocessor) strips animation/cameras/lights/colliders and keeps materials in the FBX.
- Loot: `LootDefinition.Model` (+ `ModelYaw`). `LootModelBuilder.AssignMissing` (in rebuild) gives
  definitions without a model their Kenney model and resizes `Size` to hug it (keeping the authored
  largest side). Only empty definitions are filled, so a model chosen in the inspector is kept.
  `LootPrefabGenerator` keeps the Size box as the collider (renderer removed) and fits the model
  inside it (`ModelFit.Place`). Items without a model keep their placeholder shape.
- 13 new loot items (household + electronics) with models and spawn tags; the catalog grew to match.
- Mall set dressing: `MallProps` puts themed Kenney fixtures (shelves, counters, desks, sofas,
  plants, boxes) against store walls, in a 0.85 m strip clear of loot points, doors, exterior openings,
  flights and jackpot tiles; seeded by tile. Each has one box collider and a `SectionProp` that hides it
  when its floor collapses and shows it again on restore (cosmetic, not networked, no load).
- Vehicles: the HQ van is dressed with the Car Kit van (box keeps its collider + HqVan); two parked
  vehicles in the mall lot. The mall's company truck stays greybox for now: its walk-in cargo bay and
  ramp are gameplay geometry and no CC0 model matches them.
- `NetworkLootHitTests.ClientCarriedItemStrikingRestingLootDamagesAndPushesItOnTheHost` now checks the
  largest movement after the strike: the modelled TV (deeper box) rocks back onto its base instead of
  sliding, so its final position is unchanged although the push works. `LootStrikes.PushDirection`
  now pushes along the floor (a hit on an item's top used to push it down into the floor).
- Screenshots: `M7_*` (store interiors, parking, loot lineup via `ArtPreview`).

### 7.2 notes (lighting)
- Decision: realtime lights only, no lightmap bake. Power-off contracts switch the lights per run,
  collapsed floors would leave baked light/shadow behind, and runs restart in place. Forward+ (already
  the PC renderer) takes ~100 unshadowed point lights.
- `MallFixtures`: ~95 ceiling fixtures (one per ~2x2 tiles of every zone; none under the atrium void or
  over stairs). Each = emissive panel + point light + `LightFixture`, hung from the slab above with a
  `SectionProp` (top floor: from the roof), so it falls with its ceiling and returns on restore.
  Stores warm, concourses/back rooms fluorescent; 12% faulty (flicker), 5% dead.
- `LightFixture` (Structure): lit = powered && ceiling up && not dead; drives light intensity and the
  panel's emission (MaterialPropertyBlock). `LightFixture.Disturb(s)` makes every fixture stutter;
  DangerDirector calls it on a danger level-up and keeps its own flicker for other lights.
  PowerController switches fixtures and scales the gradient ambient (`darkAmbientScale`). F1 shows
  power, lit/faulty fixtures and fog density.
- `LevelAtmosphere` (Mall + HQ moods): trilight ambient, exp2 fog, sun with soft shadows, a global
  volume `Art/Lighting/<Level>_Volume.asset` (ACES, bloom, vignette, grain, colour adjustments), and
  post-processing on the scene cameras. Assets are updated in place (stable GUIDs).
- Dust: a lit particle box per zone + a tall one in the atrium (only visible where light falls).
  Daylight: the real sun through the skylight and openings, plus a fading additive shaft mesh under
  the skylight (`Mall_SkylightShaft.asset`).
- Flashlight: soft shadows. Carried items (single or shared) stop casting shadows (Grabbable), or the
  item in your hands would black out your own beam.
- Tests: `MallLightingTests` (fixtures everywhere and mostly lit, dead ones dark, power/ceiling
  switching, disturbance dip); `MallDangerTests` now watches a working fixture.

### 7.3 notes (audio)
- Kenney CC0 Impact Sounds, Interface Sounds, RPG Audio; only the files used are imported
  (Art/ThirdParty/Kenney/<Pack>/, each with License.txt). `ThirdPartyAudioImport`: mono, decompress on
  load, Vorbis 0.6.
- `SoundBank` (Data/Audio/Resources/SoundBank.asset): named cues (`SoundId`) plus impacts (light/heavy
  by volume) and footsteps per `SurfaceMaterial`. Each `SoundCue` = clip variations, volume, pitch
  spread, min/max distance. `SoundBankBuilder` (in rebuild) only fills cues/materials that have no
  clips, so inspector edits survive. A cue without clips plays the synthesised placeholder: kept on
  purpose for the Blind One's click, horn, shriek, radio static and the collapse rumble (no CC0 sound
  fits; their signatures were designed). A crash layers library debris over the rumble.
- `GameAudio` is the single entry point (replaces PlaceholderAudio.Play* and PlayClipAtPoint). It plays
  through `AudioPool` (32 reusable sources, linear rolloff, oldest stolen) at `AudioLevels.Sfx`.
  `AudioLevels` = master/effects/ambience volumes in PlayerPrefs for the M7.4 settings menu.
- New cues on every machine: loot grab/pocket (NetworkLoot hold change), flashlight click, truck
  lever (run enters Honking), the Collector jingling while it carries loot, payday coins (appraisal
  screen), UI cues ready for the menus.
- `LevelAmbience` (mall + HQ): synthesised looping wind and mains hum (`AmbienceSynth`, crossfaded
  loops); the hum follows the share of lit ceiling fixtures (power off = no hum), and distant settling
  sounds (metal, never a creak: creaks are structural warnings). F1 shows wind/hum levels.
- Test isolation: StructurePresentationTests now clean the world first (a Mall test straight before
  them left the mall's sections playing sounds into their asserts; only visible in filtered runs).

### 7.4a notes (menus)
- UI Toolkit (built-in module; no package), built in C# (`Scripts/UI/Menus`): `MenuUi` on the session
  object (DDOL with the NetworkBootstrap) owns one `UIDocument` (`Data/UI/MenuPanel.asset`, theme
  `Art/UI/MenuTheme.tss` = default theme + `Menu.uss`, scaled from 1920x1080). Font: Kenney Future (CC0).
- Screens: offline = main menu (play solo/host, Direct IP / Steam, join, settings, credits, quit; the
  session-end notice). In a game, Esc = pause menu (resume, settings, CREW = the lobby screen with
  players, Steam lobby members, invite, the port friends join; leave / end game, quit). The game keeps
  running. Settings and credits stack on top; Esc/Back pops. Menus claim the mouse (CursorOwner);
  Resume asks the player's look to take it back (`CursorOwner.RequestCapture`). UI sounds from the
  SoundBank.
- Replaced the OnGUI NetworkPanel and VoiceSettingsPanel (deleted). NetworkValidation now checks MenuUi
  wiring (bootstrap, lobby, panel settings).
- Credits: `Data/UI/Credits.txt`, regenerated each rebuild from Docs/ASSET_CREDITS.md (+ technology).
- Batch mode never reaches `WaitForEndOfFrame`: menu screenshots render the camera by hand, then let the
  panel draw into the same RenderTexture.
- HQ brightened (ambient + lamps): it read too dark behind the menu.

### 7.4b notes (settings)
- `GameSettings` (Core, PlayerPrefs, cached, `Changed` event): sensitivity multiplier (0.25-3x), FOV
  (60-100, default 75), head bob, camera shake, subtitles (off by default), colourblind scanner.
  Designer tunables stay in FeelSettings/PlayerMovementConfig; these only scale or switch them.
- Applied by PlayerLook (sensitivity), `PlayerFieldOfView` (new, on the Player prefab, live lens),
  PlayerCameraFeel (bob/shake), StressScannerHud (Okabe-Ito blue/orange/vermilion).
- Subtitles: `SubtitleFeed` captions only warnings and threat signatures (creak, groan, snap, collapse,
  clicking, jingling, horn, shriek, static) within the cue's hearing distance, with a direction from the
  listener ("- behind, above"). `SubtitleView` (in the menu document) shows the newest 4 for 2.5 s; a
  repeat refreshes instead of stacking.

### 7.4c notes (rebinding)
- `InputBindings` (Core): overrides JSON in PlayerPrefs; every AbandonedInput instance applies them
  (PlayerInputReader at Awake and live on `Changed`). Rebindable: the Gameplay map except Look and Pause
  (Esc stays the menu key); Move's four directions separately. `ConflictWith` names a clash (warned,
  not blocked). `Display(action)` feeds HUD prompts ("[E] Pick up" follows the player's key).
- `ControlsView` (Settings -> Keys...): interactive rebinding on its own action copy, Esc cancels (and
  that Esc doesn't also close the screen), reset to defaults.
- Tests share the editor's PlayerPrefs: resets now `PlayerPrefs.Save()` (an unflushed delete came back
  and left a test's G binding in the developer's prefs; removed). The generated AbandonedInput.Dispose
  uses Destroy, so EditMode tests free its asset with DestroyImmediate.

### 7.5 notes (cosmetics)
- Data: `CosmeticDefinition` assets (coverall colour or hat prefab, unlock: runs played / runs survived /
  lifetime crew haul, all must be met) in `Data/Player/Cosmetics/CosmeticCatalog` (order = network
  byte index: append only; validated). 9 coveralls, 9 hats (none, hard hat default, beanie, cap, top
  hat from primitives; traffic cone, cardboard box over the head, lamp, potted plant from Kenney CC0).
- `PlayerProfile` (local PlayerPrefs, never the host's company save: cosmetics are personal): runs,
  escapes, haul, chosen outfit. The appraisal screen records each run (never in batch mode).
- `PlayerCosmetics` (NetworkBehaviour on the Player): owner-written `CosmeticChoice`; every machine tints
  the coverall (renderers using the player material, incl. ragdoll parts) and puts the hat on the body's
  crown or the ragdoll head. The wearer's own hat is ShadowsOnly.
- HQ: lockers on the garage's west wall (`WardrobeLocker`, E) open `WardrobeView` (menu document);
  locked items say what's left ("Play 3 more runs"). Done/Esc goes straight back to the game.
- Test isolation: fixtures that build a flat-ground rig (Feel, Carry, PlayerMovement, Ragdoll, LootDamage,
  ReviewRegression) now clean the world first; a leftover HQ put the new lockers in FeelTests' path.

### 7.6 notes (performance)
- `PerfOverlay` (F1, bottom line, every level): fps / frame ms (avg + worst per second), main-thread ms,
  batches, set-pass, triangles, awake rigidbodies, GC KB/frame, managed memory. ProfilerRecorders run
  only while F1 is on. This is the tool for the human 4-player profile.
- `nettest perf` (Mall, 1 host + 3 headless clients, 30 s: everyone circles the concourse, the host
  throws 24 loot items and drops 8 upper sections at 10 s). Headless = simulation + networking only.
  M7.6 on this Mac: every instance avg 16.7-16.9 ms (the 60 fps cap), p95 16.9-17.6 ms; host worst
  33 ms exactly at the 8-section collapse (one dropped frame); clients worst <= 21 ms; peak awake bodies
  202, peak debris 200 (= the existing `MaxLiveChunks` budget, which is the rigidbody cap that matters:
  resting loot sleeps). Budget: p95 <= 50 ms per instance (shared machine).
- Occlusion culling baked for the mall in the rebuild (`OcclusionBake`; data in Scenes/Mall/). Only
  never-breaking geometry occludes (walls, columns, roof, exterior); floor tiles carry no static
  flags, so a collapsed floor never hides what's under its hole.
- Render budget (`RenderPipelineSetup`, in rebuild, PC_RPAsset): sun shadows 2 cascades to 35 m (was 4 to
  50 m): about -18% batches indoors. `MallRenderBudgetTests` renders four mall views by hand (probe camera
  only): electronics 1067 batches / 57k tris (no occlusion 1207 / 61k), concourse 1746 / 76k
  (1902 / 91k), walkway 1660 / 75k (1745 / 86k), offices 1047 / 56k (1305 / 67k). Guard: < 2200.
- Not measured here: GPU time and the real 4-player rendering cost (needs a windowed run with F1) ->
  PROGRESS "Needs you".
