### 7.1 notes (art pass)
- Imports: `Art/ThirdParty/Kenney/<Pack>/` with the pack's License.txt. `ThirdPartyModelImport`
  (AssetPostprocessor) strips animation/cameras/lights/colliders and keeps materials in the FBX.
- Loot: `LootDefinition.Model` (+ `ModelYaw`). `LootModelBuilder.AssignMissing` (in rebuild) gives
  definitions without a model their Kenney model and resizes `Size` to hug it (keeping the authored
  largest side). Only empty definitions are filled, so a model chosen in the inspector is kept.
  `LootPrefabGenerator` keeps the Size box as the collider (renderer removed) and fits the model
  inside it (`ModelFit.Place`). Items without a model keep their placeholder shape.
- 13 new loot items (household + electronics) with models and spawn tags; the catalog grew to match.
- Mall set dressing: `MallProps` puts themed Kenney fixtures (shelves, counters, desks, sofas,
  plants, boxes) against store walls, in a 0.85 m strip clear of loot points, doors, exterior openings,
  flights and jackpot tiles; seeded by tile. Each has one box collider and a `SectionProp` that hides it
  when its floor collapses and shows it again on restore (cosmetic, not networked, no load).
- Vehicles: the HQ van is dressed with the Car Kit van (box keeps its collider + HqVan); two parked
  vehicles in the mall lot. The mall's company truck stays greybox for now: its walk-in cargo bay and
  ramp are gameplay geometry and no CC0 model matches them.
- `NetworkLootHitTests.ClientCarriedItemStrikingRestingLootDamagesAndPushesItOnTheHost` now checks the
  largest movement after the strike: the modelled TV (deeper box) rocks back onto its base instead of
  sliding, so its final position is unchanged although the push works. `LootStrikes.PushDirection`
  now pushes along the floor (a hit on an item's top used to push it down into the floor).
- Screenshots: `M7_*` (store interiors, parking, loot lineup via `ArtPreview`).

### 7.2 notes (lighting)
- Decision: realtime lights only, no lightmap bake. Power-off contracts switch the lights per run,
  collapsed floors would leave baked light/shadow behind, and runs restart in place. Forward+ (already
  the PC renderer) takes ~100 unshadowed point lights.
- `MallFixtures`: ~95 ceiling fixtures (one per ~2x2 tiles of every zone; none under the atrium void or
  over stairs). Each = emissive panel + point light + `LightFixture`, hung from the slab above with a
  `SectionProp` (top floor: from the roof), so it falls with its ceiling and returns on restore.
  Stores warm, concourses/back rooms fluorescent; 12% faulty (flicker), 5% dead.
- `LightFixture` (Structure): lit = powered && ceiling up && not dead; drives light intensity and the
  panel's emission (MaterialPropertyBlock). `LightFixture.Disturb(s)` makes every fixture stutter;
  DangerDirector calls it on a danger level-up and keeps its own flicker for other lights.
  PowerController switches fixtures and scales the gradient ambient (`darkAmbientScale`). F1 shows
  power, lit/faulty fixtures and fog density.
- `LevelAtmosphere` (Mall + HQ moods): trilight ambient, exp2 fog, sun with soft shadows, a global
  volume `Art/Lighting/<Level>_Volume.asset` (ACES, bloom, vignette, grain, colour adjustments), and
  post-processing on the scene cameras. Assets are updated in place (stable GUIDs).
- Dust: a lit particle box per zone + a tall one in the atrium (only visible where light falls).
  Daylight: the real sun through the skylight and openings, plus a fading additive shaft mesh under
  the skylight (`Mall_SkylightShaft.asset`).
- Flashlight: soft shadows. Carried items (single or shared) stop casting shadows (Grabbable), or the
  item in your hands would black out your own beam.
- Tests: `MallLightingTests` (fixtures everywhere and mostly lit, dead ones dark, power/ceiling
  switching, disturbance dip); `MallDangerTests` now watches a working fixture.
