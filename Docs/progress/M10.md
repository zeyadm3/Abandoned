### 10.1 notes (truck upgrades)
- `TruckUpgradeDefinition` tiers (Data/Company/TruckUpgrades/) in an append-only `TruckUpgradeCatalog`; owned ids live in
  CompanySave.unlocks (bankruptcy clears them); `CompanyNetState.TruckUpgrades` is a bitmask by catalog index.
- Shop "THE TRUCK" section: a tier shows once the one below is owned; the host checks level, tier and money.
- Effects ride on RunTerms (host): cargo capacity x Amount, honk = Amount s (RunNetState.HonkSeconds so the lever prompt
  is right on clients), armor = `RunState.Shelters` (Threat.KillWithinReach and the Blind One skip players in the
  truck's ride zone). Floodlights: `TruckFloodlights` on the truck, every machine, off the building's power.
- Tiers: Bigger Bay x1.35 (L2 $12k), Floodlights (L3 $8k), Tuned Engine 7 s (L4 $10k), Box Truck x1.75 (L5 $30k),
  V8 4 s (L7 $25k), Armored Bay (L9 $40k). Prices assume the 10.9 economy pass.

### 10.2 notes (flatbed trolley)
- EquipmentKind.Flatbed (appended), `flatbed` gear (L3 "better trolley", GDD 13). In a hand slot it sets
  `PlayerCarrier.FlatbedDrag` on every machine: Heavy and Huge items can be dragged short-handed (it doubles as a
  hand trolley). A Huge item drags at CarryConfig.HugeDragSpeedScale (0.55) of the normal drag speed.
- `SharedCarryable.CanBeDraggedUnderCrewed` is now true when ANY carrier has a trolley for the item (it used to ask
  only the first one), so a crew of two with one flatbed can move the piano.

### 10.3 notes (rope & pulley)
- `rope_pulley` gear (single use, L2): use it facing a hole; `Pulley.FindHole` walks forward from your feet (0.8-3.2 m)
  for the first spot with no floor within a step and a drop of 2 m+. The host spawns the `Pulley` NetworkObject
  (tripod + rope that stretches to the floor below); it despawns when the next run starts.
- `Core.SafeDescent`: rope columns registered on every machine. The owner's PlayerMotor caps an airborne fall at the
  rope speed (1.2 m/s) inside a column and keeps resetting the fall's peak, so the landing never ragdolls; the host
  caps host-simulated loot the same way (below every fragility's damage threshold, 2 m/s for Extreme).
- Carried loot is the carrier's physics: let go over the hole (drop) and it comes down on the rope once the host owns it.

### 10.4 notes (locked stores)
- `RollerShutter` (scene, Extraction): 6 single-door stores (Clothing, Jewelry, Security, Toys, Boutique, Offices) + the
  two front-entrance doors, placed by `MallShutters` after the NavMesh bake. Down = a blocking collider + a carving
  NavMeshObstacle; up = the curtain rolls (shrinks) into its housing. Cinema and Electronics2 are never lockable:
  each holds a fallback rope window (GDD 6.4).
- `RunShutters` (NetworkBehaviour on the RunState prefab): host rolls 2-3 locked stores from the run seed (all of them
  plus the entrance on the new **Sealed** modifier), x1.5 value for non-jackpot loot inside a store locked at the start;
  the mask is a NetworkVariable (-1 until rolled, so shutters don't flap at run start).
- E on a down shutter: bolt cutters (new gear, L2) cut the padlock in 1.6 s at noise 0.15; a crowbar takes 4 strikes
  (0.6 s apart, noise 0.75 each). Opening rattles (noise 0.45). F1 shows the mask.
- Modifiers now 11: + Sealed (L2), Hot Property (window x0.55, +1 jackpot, +40 %, L8), Condemned (stability -30 %, +1
  threat, +45 %, L10) - the GDD 13 "harder modifiers" at higher levels. Flooded basement: no basement in the mall.

### 10.5 notes (detection gear)
- `motion_detector` (L4): `MotionDetectorHud` (owner): every 0.25 s compares threats' and other players' replicated
  positions; anything faster than 0.5 m/s within 22 m is listed (distance, AHEAD/LEFT/BEHIND-RIGHT..., ABOVE/BELOW) and
  it ticks faster as the nearest closes in. Standing still hides you from it.
- `night_vision` (L6): `NightVisionGoggles` (owner): use with empty hands toggles; a runtime URP Volume (exposure +2.4,
  green filter, grain, vignette) and a 14 m IR point light that only its owner sees; 120 s battery, recharges at
  0.35 s/s while off. The runtime asmdef now references the URP runtime assemblies (already in the project).

### 10.6 notes (cosmetics)
- 15 hats (+ bucket, miner's helmet, cowboy, chef, paper crown, Kenney trash can and teddy bear), 12 coveralls (+ white,
  purple, teal), and a new accessory slot: goggles, dust mask, shades, rucksack, gas mask, moustache (+ none).
- `CosmeticChoice` carries a third byte (Accessory; With* helpers keep the other picks); the catalog's accessory list is
  append-only like the others; PlayerProfile remembers the pick. Accessories hang off the hat anchor (crown) with
  their own offsets; body-mounted ones (the rucksack) hide while their wearer is ragdolled. Your own are shadow-only.
