### 9.1 notes (the Hunter)
- `Hunter` (Threats, host only, `HunterConfig` in Data/Threats): Patrol (random reachable points within
  24 m) -> Chase (seen: 110 deg cone to 22 m, crouching players only within 9 m, anyone within 3 m; line
  of sight required) at 5.2 m/s (between walk 4.5 and sprint 7) -> Search where it last saw you (7 s) ->
  Patrol. Kills on contact. Replicated state; heavy footsteps on every machine (quicker when charging),
  captioned "[heavy footsteps]"; its own first-time tip.
- An `ILoadSource` of 450 kg at its feet (host): it cracks weak floors. When the section it stands on
  collapses it falls (from where it last stood: the collapse carves the NavMesh and the agent is pushed
  onto the next tile in the same frame, so the check is on the section, not a ray), lands with a crash
  and camera shake, lies stunned 5 s, then searches.
- Roster: Blind One 0.4, Stalker 0.2, Collector 0.2, Hunter 0.2 of run openings.
- Sound ids are appended (HunterStep, HunterLanding): the SoundBank stores ids by number.
