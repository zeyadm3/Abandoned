# Location Expansion — School, Factory, Prison

Three new hand-built contract locations join Meridian Mall.  They use the same run rules as the
Mall: a fixed readable layout, seeded loot/threat/power/structural variation, a truck outside, and
at least one map-authored route that cannot be permanently cut off by collapse.

## School

An abandoned secondary school with a classroom wing, library, gymnasium, auditorium and a flooded
service basement.  Its route is broad and legible at first, then branches through tightly packed
rooms.  The gym bleachers and the auditorium balcony are the high-risk structural showcases.
Projectors, instruments, trophy cases and science equipment make its valuable loot feel distinct.

## Factory

An old fabrication plant: receiving dock, assembly hall, boiler room, maintenance mezzanine and
loading floor.  It is the most open and vertical location.  Catwalks, light industrial platforms and
the crane-side service bridge make carrying large machinery a deliberate risk rather than a shortcut.
The loading dock gives the truck a clear, heavily defended extraction route.

## Prison

An abandoned correctional complex built around two cell blocks, intake, visitation, laundry,
kitchen and a maintenance spine.  It is the most constrained map: sightlines are long, rooms are
narrow and the safest route is often not the quickest one.  The cell-tier galleries create its
signature collapse choice; the maintenance route and ground-level yard keep extraction possible.

## Shared implementation rules

- Every location has four player spawns, a truck and collection bay, threat spawn points, seeded loot
  markers, a `StructureSimulation`, and the normal network session/run objects.
- No location adds a paid pack or Unity package.  Geometry and dressing are authored by the project
  builders using the existing materials and systems.
- The contract board chooses locations deterministically from the crew's unlocked set.  A location is
  a data definition rather than a special case in contract code.
- All three locations are immediately eligible on the board. School is the gentler route-reading option,
  Factory carries higher-value heavy salvage, and Prison is the highest-pressure route; their weighting and
  balancing remain tunable in the location catalog.
- Each scene is generated by an Editor builder; saved Unity YAML is never hand-edited.
