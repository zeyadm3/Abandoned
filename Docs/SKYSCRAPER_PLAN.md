- Downtown: **48 surrounding high-rises**, 108–396 m tall, across a persistent 720 m square district with connected avenues, podium shops, sidewalks, crossings, street lamps and traffic props.

- World integration: origin-authored scene, existing footprint and collection van marker; standalone truck and skyline remain under `Exterior/Truck` and `Exterior/Skyline` for streaming to hide.
- Authoring: shared exact stair/shaft cutouts on all decks, newly baked navigation and occlusion, scene-local sky/lighting/material finish, and twelve saved review views.

The rebuild uses the existing **Tools > Abandoned > Maps > Skyscraper Restaurant > Rebuild Skyscraper Restaurant** menu item. No additional packages are required.
