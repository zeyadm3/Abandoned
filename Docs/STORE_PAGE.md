## About this game

**Four friends. One collapsing building. A $100,000 statue that weighs two tons. What could go wrong?**

Society collapsed. You run the cheapest salvage company left. Drive out to an abandoned mall, find anything
worth money, and carry it to the truck — before the building, or whatever lives in it, gets you.

**The building is the enemy.** Every floor, stair and walkway has a load limit. Floors creak, crack, sag and
collapse under weight and impacts — and everything on them comes down too. Plan your route, spread out, and
think twice before the whole crew stands on the bridge with the piano.

**Your loot is heavy.** A grand piano needs three people on the handles. A vase is worth $25,000 until it
touches anything. Pockets for the small stuff, a trolley for the big stuff, a rope and pulley for the really
big stuff, and a truck that won't wait forever.

**Something hears you.** The Blind One hunts by sound — footsteps, dropped loot, creaking floors and your
voice chat. The Stalker waits for you to look away. The Collector steals whatever you leave lying around.
The Hunter is huge, fast, and heavier than the floor it's standing on.

**One more floor?** Every job has a quota. The truck's right outside. You can leave whenever you want. The
game is about not wanting to.

### Features
- 1–4 player online co-op (friends-only lobbies, Steam invites); playable solo
- A structural system: load, cracks, sagging and collapses you can read, plan around — and cause on purpose
- Shared carrying: lift, swing and argue a sofa around a corner together
- Proximity voice chat with wall muffling and walkie-talkies — and monsters that can hear it
- Your company: contracts with modifiers (power off, storm, sealed, rush job...), payday, debt and bankruptcy,
  gear, truck upgrades, cosmetics, achievements
- The abandoned mall: three floors, an atrium, escalators, locked stores, and a different job every run

### Early Access
**Why Early Access?** The core game is done and fun with friends; we want players shaping what comes next.
**How long?** Roughly 6–12 months.
**Full version plans:** the Hospital and the Luxury Hotel, more threats, more loot, layout variants for the
mall, more cosmetics.
**Current state:** the full company loop at the abandoned mall, 4 threats, ~57 loot items, 15+ pieces of gear,
11 contract modifiers.
**Price:** will the price change? Likely a little at full release, as content is added.
**Community:** the Discord is linked from the main menu; bug reports with your log file are one click away.
